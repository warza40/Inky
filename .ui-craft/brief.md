# Project Brief

## Design Intent

Warm-humanist editorial portfolio on a refined paper canvas — inspired by Cursor's marketing surface. Not scrapbook, not collage: a modern interface where warmth, restraint, and reading comfort carry the hierarchy. Paper texture and tonal variation stay cohesive across homepage, case studies, and section sheets.

**Aesthetic preset:** `aesthetics/warm-humanist/cursor`

## Audience

Design leaders, hiring managers, and collaborators reviewing case work on desktop first. They want clarity, craft, and evidence of systems thinking — not marketing spectacle.

## Voice and Tone

Direct and honest. First person where it fits. No filler adjectives. Errors and empty states explain plainly.

## Constraints

- Paper material system is canonical — warm canvas, fibrous texture, dot-grid on selected surfaces, sheet elevation via tone not decoration
- One accent color (`#f54e00`) for links and interactive emphasis only
- Display typography at weight 400 — authority from tracking, not boldness
- Motion: opacity and color only, 100–200ms; no transform hovers on cards
- Serif (Source Serif 4) for long-form prose; sans for UI and display; mono for metadata
